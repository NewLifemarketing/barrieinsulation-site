What we install

Insulation Services

Spray foam, blown-in BIBS, batt and insulation removal — installed across Barrie, Simcoe County and the Kawartha Lakes area by certified applicators.

Four systems, matched to the assembly

Most houses we quote end up with a combination — foam where it seals and outperforms, blown-in or batt where a cheaper product does the same job. Here is what each one is for.

Spray Foam Insulation

A seamless air barrier and insulation in one step — the highest-performing option for attics, basements, crawl spaces and hard-to-seal areas.

Learn about spray foam →

Blown-In (BIBS) Insulation

The Blow-In Blanket System packs cavities completely full — R-24 in a 2x6 wall, R-14 in a 2x4 — with excellent sound control and a drill-and-fill option for finished walls.

Learn about blown-in →

BATT Insulation

Traditional fibreglass and mineral wool batts, installed properly — friction-fit, uncompressed and detailed to the Ontario Building Code.

Learn about batt →

Insulation Removal

HEPA vacuum removal of old, contaminated, pest-damaged or water-damaged insulation — then we fix what caused it and re-insulate to code.

Learn about removal →

Spray foam vs. blown-in vs. batt

The honest differences, including where the expensive option is not worth paying for.

How the three insulation systems compare
Spray FoamBlown-In (BIBS)BATT
R-value per inchHighest (about R-6/in)MediumMedium
Air barrierYes — seals as it expandsNoNo
Vapour retarderYes, at normal thicknessNoNo
Fills irregular cavitiesCompletelyCompletelyOnly if cut and fitted well
Settling over timeNone — bonded in placeNone — blown to densityCan sag or gap if poorly fitted
Sound controlGoodExcellentGood (best with mineral wool)
Works in finished wallsNoYes — drill-and-fillNo
Relative costHighestMiddleLowest
Best used forAttics, rim joists, crawl spaces, cathedral ceilings, retrofitsWalls, attics, sound walls, drill-and-fillOpen, standard, dry framing in new builds
