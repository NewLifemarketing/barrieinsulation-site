Credentials

What we are certified to do

Insulation is one of the few trades where the difference between good and bad work is invisible the day it is finished. Certification is how you check before you find out the hard way.

CAN/ULC-S705.2 certified applicators

The Canadian standard for the on-site installation of rigid polyurethane spray foam. It requires trained, certified installers, controlled site conditions, documented lift thicknesses and daily quality records.

Ontario Building Code compliant

Every product is specified and installed to meet the requirements that apply to your build type and permit — so the work passes inspection the first time.

Certified SPFI contractor · CUFCA member

A certified Sprayed Polyurethane Foam Insulation (SPFI) contractor and a member of CUFCA — the Canadian Urethane Foam Contractors Association, the national body that sets and audits installation quality standards for this trade.

The result

What you actually get out of it

Lower energy bills

Heating and cooling is the largest line on an Ontario utility bill, and in an under-insulated house a large share of it goes straight outside. Sealing and insulating properly is the one home improvement that starts paying you back the day it is finished.

A healthier, more comfortable home

Air sealing stops the drafts, the cold floors and the room that is always five degrees off. It also cuts the moisture movement that leads to condensation, mould and ice damming.

A smaller footprint

Energy you never use is the cleanest energy there is. Less fuel burned every winter for the life of the building — and our spray foam uses soya-based renewable content in place of part of the petroleum-based resin.

Certified, code-compliant work

Spray foam installed to CAN/ULC-S705.2 by certified applicators, and every product specified to meet the Ontario Building Code requirements for your build.

Questions worth asking any insulation contractor

Ours are answered above. Ask the next quote the same ones.

  • Are your spray foam applicators certified to CAN/ULC-S705.2? What is the certification number?
  • Will the quote be itemized by area and product, with the reasoning for each?
  • What thermal barrier does the code require over the foam in my space, and is it in the price?
  • What is the re-entry time after spraying, and who confirms it?
  • If my attic insulation is contaminated, do you remove it or insulate over it?
  • Who is liable if the work does not pass inspection?
