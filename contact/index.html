Before you call

What does the quote process look like?

Call or send the form, we book a visit, and we get into the attic, basement or crawl space to see what is actually there. You get a written, itemized quote with the reasoning behind each recommendation. No obligation, no pressure, and no charge for the visit.

How long does the work take?

Most residential jobs are one to two days. An attic removal and re-insulation is typically a day for each. Large new builds and commercial work are scheduled around your other trades. We give you a realistic window rather than an optimistic one.

Do you serve my area?

We work throughout Barrie and Simcoe County — Innisfil, Orillia, Wasaga Beach, Alliston, Angus, Midland, Collingwood, Bradford and the surrounding townships — as well as the Kawartha Lakes area. If you are not sure whether you are in range, call us; if we cannot get to you we will usually know someone who can.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 7:30 am to 5:30 pm. We are closed Saturday and Sunday. Quote requests submitted through the website on a weekend are answered the next business day.
