Local, and local for a long time

Fifteen years in one region means we know how the buildings here fail — the snow-belt winters, the lake-effect wind, the century farmhouses, the cottages being pushed into four-season use, and the subdivisions that were built fast.

Barrie

Barrie is home. It is where we are based, where most of our work is, and where we have spent more than 15 years insulating everything from post-war bungalows in the older east end to new subdivisions on the south side.
